# Chiman: The Great Bloom

A hyper-casual 3D jumper for **Apple Watch, iPhone and iPad**.

Chiman is a sack of burlap with a garden growing out of his head. He lives on
flowerpots that float in a warm evening sky, and the only way onward is up.

Hold to charge a jump. Release on the mark. Land clean and your streak climbs;
hold too briefly and he falls short, too long and he sails past.

- **One input.** Hold, release. That is the whole game.
- **Built for the wrist.** A real 3D world running on Apple Watch, and it runs
  there on its own, with no phone nearby.
- **Catch the dew.** Chiman dries out as he climbs. Miss too many drops and he
  withers.
- **Mind the shears.** Some pots come with garden shears on their own schedule.
- **No last level.** Only your best run, and the next one.

## Support

Found a bug, or something feels wrong? **[Open an issue](../../issues)** — that
is the fastest way to reach me, and it lets other players see the answer too.

Helpful things to include:

- Which device (Apple Watch, iPhone, iPad) and which OS version
- What you were doing when it happened
- A screenshot, if the problem is something you can see
