# Privacy Policy — Chiman: The Great Bloom

_Last updated: August 2026_

Chiman is designed with your privacy in mind. **The app does not collect,
store, transmit, or share any personal data.**

## What we collect

**Nothing.** Chiman does not collect any personal information or usage data.

## Data stored on your device

- Your **high score**, your **best run length**, and your **sound on/off
  preference** are saved **locally on your device only**, in a single file
  inside the app. This information never leaves your device and is not
  accessible to us or any third party.

## What we don't do

- No accounts or sign-in
- No analytics or tracking
- No advertising
- No third-party SDKs
- No in-app purchases
- No network requests — the game runs entirely offline

## Children's privacy

Because Chiman collects no data of any kind, it collects no data from children.
